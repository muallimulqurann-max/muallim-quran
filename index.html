Help & frequently asked questions

Quick answers to help you study with confidence.

Can’t find what you need? Review the guidance below or contact your platform administrator.

How do I start learning?

Open Books, choose a book and unit, then select an available lesson. Use the lesson materials and complete its activity.

How do I unlock the next lesson?

Complete the activity for the current lesson. Lessons may unlock in sequence, so follow the prompts shown on the lesson page.

Where can I see my progress?

Open Progress to view your overall completion, completed units, lesson activity results and review options.

Can I repeat a lesson or activity?

Yes. Use Review or open a completed lesson to revisit its material and retake activities when available.

Is my progress saved?

Your learning progress is saved to your account when you are signed in and the platform can connect to its data service. If something looks out of date, refresh the page and check your connection.

What if a video or PDF does not open?

Check your internet connection, allow embedded content if your browser blocks it, and try refreshing the page. If it still fails, contact your platform administrator.

How do I change the text size or theme?

Open Settings, choose your preferred appearance options, and select Save appearance. Preferences are stored in this browser.

How do I sign out safely?

Use Log out in the top-right corner. Always sign out when using a shared or public computer.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Use the password recovery option provided by your account administrator or the sign-in service configured for this platform.
